# Decisions index

> **Purpose:** one line per decision in [decisions.md](decisions.md), to find the entry that explains a pattern. Read the matching entry by its D-number (`### D<n>:` heading) or search a topic tag here; **never load decisions.md whole** (about 110K tokens).

How this index was made: generated by script from the entries, not written by hand. **Summary** is the entry's own first sentence of its Decision (cut at 165 characters). **Tags** are keyword matches on the title and summary: use them to search, not as classification. **Status** is one of three values, taken only from what `decisions.md` itself says: `superseded by Dn` (an entry says the whole decision is replaced), `active` (a later entry says it stands), or `unclear` (the text does not say; **unclear is not "active"**, and most entries are unclear because `decisions.md` has no status field). "PARTLY" in the last column marks an entry whose text names a later entry that replaces or amends part of it. To change a decision, amend its entry (or add one that supersedes it) and update this line. The review lists and counts are in `docs/_reorg/decision-status.md`.
