# ISS-031: The grid cells are bigger than Figma's

- Severity: Low
- Type: Gap
- Status: Open
- Audit rows: SN1 (grid)
- Found: 2026-11-13 (owner, by eye)

## Steps
1. Open Studio at 100% and look at the grid.
2. Open Figma at 100% with the grid on and compare the cell size.

## Expected (Figma)
Small cells (Figma's grid is fine, about 10 px, and shows more lines as you zoom in).

## Actual (Studio)
Cells are bigger. The grid is 25 cm per cell (`GRID_CM = 25`, `geometry.ts` line 9), drawn as the minor pattern with a 100 cm major block in `FloorPlanCanvas.vue` (~line 1108).

## Evidence
`evidence/ISS-031/figma-grid-owner.png` (Figma). Add a Studio screenshot at the same zoom when we retest.

## Suspected cause
The drawn grid and the snap step are the same constant (25 cm). One value drives both the look and the move step.

## Fix direction
Separate the two ideas: the drawn grid size and the snap step. Draw a finer minor grid (for example 10 cm) and keep 100 cm as the major line. Decide the snap step on its own (see ISS-004). Optional, like Figma: show the finer lines only above some zoom. Owner decision needed: the exact minor size.

## How to verify
By eye at 100% next to Figma: cell size looks the same. Moving still snaps as decided in ISS-004.

## Figma research (2026-11-13)
- Figma's pixel grid is 1 px squares and only shows at zoom 400% or higher (Figma help: "Adjust your zoom and view options").
- Figma's layout grid is user-set; the default uniform grid is 10 px (Figma help: "Create layout guides"; many teams change it to 8).
- Studio draws 1 cm as 1 screen px at 100% (D278), so 10 cm matches Figma's 10 px default.

## Recommendation
Minor grid 10 cm, major 100 cm (already drawn). Fade the minor lines out when zoomed out so they do not become a dense texture. Keep the snap step a separate decision (ISS-004).
