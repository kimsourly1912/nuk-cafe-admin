# ISS-009: A yellow ring shows around the whole editor after clicking the canvas

- Severity: Low
- Type: Improvement
- Status: Open (owner decided 2026-11-13)
- Check ID: SE3, PN3

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open Studio 2D. Click the plan, then use keys (Escape, Ctrl+A, Tab).

## Expected (Figma)
No ring around the canvas. Selection is shown only by the object's outline and handles.

## Actual (Studio)
A thin yellow border appears around the full editor area. It is probably the focus ring of the canvas (`tabindex="0"` at `FloorPlanCanvas.vue` line 1094) shown because keys were used. Not confirmed with a plain mouse click, because my test tool sends keys.

## Evidence
| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| evidence/ISS-009/studio-01-yellow-ring-around-editor.jpg | The ring around the whole editor with 10 items selected |

## Suspected cause
Browser `:focus-visible` outline on the canvas element.

## Fix direction
Owner decision (2026-11-13): remove the ring and follow Figma. No ring around the editor or canvas for mouse or keyboard focus; the selected object's outline and handles are the focus cue.

## How to verify the fix
Click the canvas, then use Escape, Ctrl+A and Tab: no yellow ring appears around the editor. Form fields and buttons in the panels keep their normal focus ring.
