# ISS-034: Elements and walls do not stick together; gaps and broken corners

- Severity: High
- Type: Bug
- Status: Open
- Audit rows: SN (snapping), ST3
- Found: 2026-11-13 (owner, with 2 screenshots)

## Steps
1. Move the stairs (or any piece) to the wall. Try to make them touch.
2. Look at the four wall corners on the plan.

## Expected (Figma)
Objects snap edge to edge to other objects with no gap (and to equal gaps). Walls that meet make a clean corner.

## Actual (Studio)
- A piece cannot be placed touching a neighbour or a wall: a gap stays (stairs next to the wall).
- At each wall corner there is a notch or an overlap: one wall is shorter or longer, so the corner looks broken (circled in red).

## Evidence
`evidence/ISS-034/stairs-gap-at-wall-owner.png`, `evidence/ISS-034/wall-corner-breaks-owner.png`.

## Suspected cause
- Gap: move snapping uses the 25 cm grid plus guides (8 px). Guides align centers and edges of pieces, but the wall is drawn with thickness 20 cm centered on its line (`WALL_THICKNESS_CM`, `shift = thickness/2` at line 214), so the inner face of the wall is not on a grid line or on a guide target.
- Corners: each wall is a separate line with `stroke-linecap="square"` (lines 1236, 1521) and no join, so walls with different angles or thickness cannot make a clean miter.

## Fix direction
1. Snap edges to the wall faces (inner and outer face) and to piece edges, not only to the wall center line.
2. Draw connected walls as one path with `stroke-linejoin="miter"` or compute the corner points, so corners are clean at any angle. Use `butt` caps for free ends.
3. Owner decision: should pieces snap to each other edge to edge by default (like Figma)? Owner said yes.
4. Unit tests for the edge targets and a visual check of corners at 0, 45 and 90 degrees.

## How to verify
Move the stairs to the wall: it snaps flush with the wall face. All four corners in the screenshot look clean. Two pieces can be placed touching.
