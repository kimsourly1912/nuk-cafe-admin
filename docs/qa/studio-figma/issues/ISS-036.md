# ISS-036: Left sidebar should list pages, and each floor should be a frame

- Severity: Medium
- Type: Improvement (product change)
- Status: Needs owner decision
- Audit rows: new (Pages and frames), PN
- Found: 2026-11-13 (owner)

## Owner request
Today the left sidebar lists the floors (Ground, Rooftop, Second, Third) and, under the active floor, its pieces. The owner suggests that each floor becomes a frame, and the left sidebar lists pages, like Figma: a page contains frames, and the layers list shows what is in the selected frame.

## Expected (Figma)
- Left panel: Pages list on top, Layers list below.
- A page holds many frames on one infinite canvas. You pan to a frame, or press Shift+2 to zoom to it.
- Layers shows frames and their children as a tree.

## Actual (Studio)
- One floor is shown at a time. The sidebar is a tree: floors, with the pieces of the active floor under it.
- Switching floor replaces the canvas (also a dropdown at the top left).
- Evidence: `evidence/ISS-036/left-sidebar-floors-owner.png`.

## Questions for the owner (do not guess)
1. What is a page: a building, a "section" of the cafe, or only a way to group floors? What are the default pages?
2. On one page, are all floors visible at once on one canvas (stacked or side by side)? The 3D view and "show floor below" (`showBelow`) assume one floor at a time.
3. Is the floor-to-floor order meaningful (Ground, Second, Third) in 3D and for stairs? Frames on a free canvas lose the order unless it is kept as data.
4. Is this a data change (new `pages` list in the layout) or only a view change? A data change needs a migration and the integrity rules in the data-model docs.

## Fix direction (after the decision)
Do it in small steps, each its own PR: (1) layers panel shows frames and children as a tree with Figma behavior (ISS-021); (2) the floor becomes a frame object with move (ISS-035); (3) pages list in the sidebar, pages stored in the layout with a version; (4) multi-frame canvas and 3D handling. A big rewrite in one PR is not recommended because the editor, 3D view and tables all depend on the one-floor-at-a-time model.

## How to verify
Create two pages with frames, switch pages, move frames, reload: structure and positions are kept; 3D view and table QR still work.
