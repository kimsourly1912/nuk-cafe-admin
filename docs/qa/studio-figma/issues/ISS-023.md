# ISS-023: Gap distances (Alt) show but are not as smooth as Figma

- Severity: Low
- Type: Improvement
- Status: Open (details needed) (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
- Audit rows: SN2
- Found: 2026-11-13 (owner by hand, T98)

## Start state
Studio, a piece selected, another piece on the plan.

## Steps
1. Select one piece.
2. Hold Alt and hover another piece, then move the pointer between pieces and to a wall.
3. Compare with Figma: select an object, hold Alt and hover another object.

## Expected (Figma)
Red distance lines and numbers follow the hovered object at once, update while the pointer moves, and show on key press without moving the mouse.

## Actual (Studio)
Owner: the gaps do show, but not as smoothly as Figma. Exact symptom not given yet (late update, flicker, only after a pointer move, different lines).
From the code: gaps are measured from the pointer-move handler (`FloorPlanCanvas.vue`, around line 325, `gapsBetween`), so pressing or releasing Alt without moving the mouse probably does not update them (same cause as ISS-017).

## Evidence
Test log T98.

## Suspected cause
Gaps are computed in pointer move only; no key event re-runs them. Not verified in detail.

## Fix direction
Recompute the gaps on `keydown`/`keyup` for Alt as well as on pointer move (same live-key mechanism as ISS-017). Ask the owner what exactly feels not smooth.

## How to verify
By hand: hold Alt without moving the mouse and the gaps appear; release and they disappear; moving between pieces updates at once.

## Open questions
- What exactly feels different from Figma?

## Work (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
