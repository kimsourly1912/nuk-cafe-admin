# ISS-010: Multi-selection panel button shows "Zoom to s..." (cut-off text)

- Severity: Low
- Type: Bug
- Status: Fixed, needs retest (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
- Check ID: IN1, SE2

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open Studio 2D in a 1568 px wide window. Click one table, Shift+click another.
2. Look at the buttons in the right panel under "2 selected".

## Expected
The full label "Zoom to selection" or a shorter label that fits, with a tooltip.

## Actual
The label is cut to "Zoom to s…" because the three buttons share a narrow row. Same in the 10-item selection.

## Evidence
| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| evidence/ISS-010/studio-01-zoom-to-s-truncated.jpg | Panel with "Rotate 90°", "Duplicate", "Zoom to s…", "Delete" |

## Suspected cause
Fixed button grid in the multi-selection panel. Probably the multi-selection panel in `DesignShopEditor.vue` (buttons around line 1710, `label="Rotate 90°"`). Not confirmed.

## Fix direction
Use a short label ("Zoom") with the full text as tooltip, or let the buttons wrap. Check light and dark theme and a narrower window.

## How to verify the fix
Select two items at 1280 and 1568 px width: no label is cut off.

## Work (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
The several-selected button is "Zoom" with "Zoom to selection" as tooltip and accessible name.
