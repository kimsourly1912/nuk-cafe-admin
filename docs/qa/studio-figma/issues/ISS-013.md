# ISS-013: Rotate should be free by default and Shift should snap to 15 degrees, like Figma

- Severity: Low
- Type: Improvement
- Status: Fixed, needs retest (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
- Check ID: TR5
- Test log: T49, T50

## Start state
Studio 2D, a table selected, rotate handle visible (the circle on a short stem next to the box).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Drag the rotate handle around the table.
2. Repeat with Shift held.

## Expected (Figma)
Rotation is free by default. Shift snaps to 15 degree steps. The angle shows while rotating. (From the Audit's general-knowledge expectation; the Figma side was not run here.)

## Actual (Studio)
Dragging the handle to a point straight below a table turned P02 from 90 to 180 degrees (T49). By the code, the angle snaps to 15 degree steps by default and Shift makes it free: `FloorPlanCanvas.vue` about line 703, `normalizeRotation(event.shiftKey ? angle : Math.round(angle / 15) * 15)`. This is the reverse of Figma. The app's shortcut list has no entry for this key. Not tested with Shift in the browser (my tool may not send Shift on drags).

## Evidence
| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| evidence/ISS-013/studio-01-rotate-handle-result.jpg | P02 at 180 degrees after the handle drag |

## Suspected cause
A design choice: for a floor plan, snapping by default keeps tables square to the walls.

## Fix direction
Owner decision (2026-11-13): follow Figma, not the current Studio behavior. Reason: users will later design their own shop freely, like in Roblox, so free rotation is the right default. Change the rotate gesture so the angle is free by default and Shift snaps to 15 degrees (swap the two branches at `FloorPlanCanvas.vue` about line 703). Update the doc comment at line 8, `STUDIO_DRAG_KEYS` in `studio.ts` and the matching e2e or unit tests. Show the angle while rotating. Related question for the owner, not decided: with free design as the goal, should the default 25 cm move snap and 5 cm Ctrl step also become free by default later?

## How to verify the fix
Drag the rotate handle: the angle follows the pointer freely. Hold Shift: it snaps to 15 degree steps. The shortcut list shows the key and the angle shows while rotating.

## Work (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
Turning is free by default; Shift snaps to 15°. Shortcut list updated. e2e.
