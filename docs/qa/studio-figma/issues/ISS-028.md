# ISS-028: The canvas breaks when a side panel hides or shows

- Severity: High
- Type: Bug
- Status: Open
- Audit rows: PN2
- Found: 2026-11-13 (T112, T116, T121; owner confirmed Figma keeps the view and Studio "breaks the canvas")

## Start state
Studio, Ground floor in 2D, any zoom (tested at 77% and 196%). Select any object.

## Steps
1. Note the zoom label and where the plan sits.
2. Click the panel toggle at the top left (left panel). The left panel hides.
3. Click the toggle again to show the panel.

## Expected (Figma, owner confirmed)
The canvas does not change. The zoom and the plan position stay the same. The canvas just gets wider or narrower, and what is on screen stays where it was.

## Actual (Studio)
After hiding the left panel (196% test):
- The whole plan jumps about 90 px to the left. The stairs are cut off at the left edge.
- The zoom label can change (77% became 72% in T112).
- A blank grey strip appears at the right edge of the canvas, before the inspector.
- The floating toolbar and the floor dropdown move to new positions.
After showing the panel again, the layout comes back to normal.

## Suspected cause (read from code)
`FloorPlanCanvas.vue`: the view is stored as a `viewBox` in cm (`view = {x, y, width, height}`), and `scale` is computed from the canvas size with `Math.min(screenWidth / view.width, screenHeight / view.height)` (`xMidYMid meet`). When the canvas width changes, `scale` changes and the SVG re-centers the box, so the plan moves and the zoom label changes. Also `perPixel` follows the width, so handles and labels resize. The view is not anchored to a fixed plan point. The blank strip and cut-off likely come from the SVG and its overlays measuring the old width for one frame, or from a container that is not resized together with the SVG (not confirmed, needs a look in the browser).

## Fix direction
Keep the zoom (screen pixels per cm) and one anchor point (same plan point at the canvas top-left, like Figma) as the source of truth, instead of a viewBox that is re-fitted to the canvas size. On resize, change only the visible width and height, never the scale or the anchor. Fit only on first load of a floor and on the Fit command. Make sure the SVG and all overlays (guides, selection box, toolbar) use the same measured size. Add a unit test for the view math ("resize keeps scale and anchor").

## How to verify
By hand: hide and show the left panel and the inspector, and resize the window. The zoom label and the plan position do not change, nothing is cut off, no blank strip appears. Check at 77% and 196%. Test for the pure function.
