# ISS-028: Zoom and position change when a side panel hides or shows

- Severity: Medium
- Type: Bug
- Status: Open
- Audit rows: PN2
- Found: 2026-11-13 (T112, owner confirmed Figma keeps the zoom, T116)

## Start state
Studio, Ground floor in 2D, zoom 77%.

## Steps
1. Note the zoom label and the plan position.
2. Click the panel toggle at the top left to hide the left panel, then show it again.

## Expected (Figma, owner confirmed)
The zoom and the pan position stay exactly the same when a panel hides, shows or resizes.

## Actual (Studio)
The zoom label went from 77% to 72% and the plan moved. The view is probably re-fitted to the new canvas size each time.

## Evidence
Test log T112, T116.

## Suspected cause
Not read yet. Likely a resize observer or a "fit to screen" run when the canvas width changes in `FloorPlanCanvas.vue` or `DesignShopEditor.vue`.

## Fix direction
Fit the view only on the first load of a floor and on the Fit command. On a canvas resize, keep the zoom (scale) and keep the same plan point at the canvas center (or top-left, pick one and test it). Add a unit test for the view math ("resize keeps scale and center").

## How to verify
By hand: hide and show the panel, resize the window: zoom label and plan position do not change. Test for the pure function.
