# ISS-033: A rotate circle floats above the element (Figma has none)

- Severity: Medium
- Type: Gap
- Status: Fixed, needs retest (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
- Audit rows: TR (rotate), IN (handles)
- Found: 2026-11-13 (owner)

## Steps
1. Select a table or piece. Look above it.

## Expected (Figma)
No separate rotate handle. You rotate by moving the pointer just outside a corner (the cursor turns into the rotate cursor). You resize by dragging the corner or the side. While rotating, a live angle label shows.

## Actual (Studio)
A circle on a stem sits above the element (`data-handle="rotate"`, `data-testid="rotate-handle"`, `cy = -depth/2 - 56`, `FloorPlanCanvas.vue` ~line 1890 and ~1959; its point is also part of the selection frame at line 379). It takes space and is not like Figma.

## Evidence
`evidence/ISS-032/studio-ring-far-from-block-owner.png` shows the circle above the Block.

## Fix direction
Remove the circle. Add a rotate zone just outside each corner handle (invisible hit area, about 12 to 16 px, rotate cursor), starting the same `rotate` gesture. Keep the Shift 15 degree step (ISS-013, owner wants Figma free by default). Show a live angle label. Update the tests that look for `rotate-handle`. Related: ISS-029 (live label), ISS-030 (cursors).

## How to verify
No circle on select. Pointer just outside a corner shows the rotate cursor and rotating works. Resize still works from corners and sides.

## Work (branch `claude/bold-hypatia-sx8nkl`, 2026-11-13)
The circle is gone. A 20 px zone just outside each corner (past the resize handle) turns the piece by as much as the pointer turns (`rotationFromDrag`, unit tested); the angle shows beside the pointer. e2e turns L1 from a corner. The rotate cursor is still the browser's `alias` (ISS-030).
