# ISS-046: Outer walls and the floor surface cannot be deleted

- Severity: Medium
- Type: Gap
- Status: Fixed, needs retest (2026-11-14, branch `ccr-e78bff39-u70ma7`)
- Check ID: none
- Found: 2026-11-14, owner by hand + Claude (code review), `main` at `06bf118`

## Start state
Studio, a floor drawn from a starter.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Select an outer wall and press Delete.
2. In Edit points, try to delete corners until 3 are left, then one more.
3. Try to delete the floor surface while keeping the walls, or the walls while keeping the surface.

## Expected (reference: Revit, The Sims)
Walls and the floor surface are separate objects. Any wall, corner, opening or surface can really be deleted.

## Actual
- Owner (2026-11-14): "can't delete it part".
- From code: Delete on an outer wall only sets it to `open` (and drops its doors); the side stays part of the outline. A corner cannot be deleted below 3. The floor surface and outer walls are the same outline, so neither can go alone. Outer walls (`edges[]`) and inside walls (`walls[]`) are two different systems.

## Suspected cause
- `app/features/branches/components/DesignShopEditor.vue`, `remove()` (line 492): an edge becomes `open`.
- `app/features/branches/components/FloorPlanCanvas.vue` line 1356: `outline.length > 3`.
- `shared/contracts/shop-layout.ts`, `floorSchema`: one outline, one `edges[]` entry per side.
A model choice, not a slip.

## Fix direction
Contract v2 in the [plan](../../../plans/studio-builder.md) (Phase 1): separate `slabs[]` and one `walls[]` list (outer and inside merged), openings hosted on walls. Deleting a wall deletes its openings after a warning (owner decision D6 in the plan); the street door stays guarded (ISS-038). One `upgradeFloor()` turns old floors into the new shape on load and on version restore.

## How to verify the fix
Each step above deletes the part for real, as one undo step; the layout saves and reloads; 2D and 3D agree.

## Open questions
Deleting a wall with doors: delete its doors too after a warning (recommended), or refuse?

## Owner decisions
Owner (2026-11-14, plan D6): deleting a wall with doors deletes them silently; Undo brings them back. Owner (2026-11-14): build the full contract v2 (slabs and one wall list), not the smaller variant Claude offered.
