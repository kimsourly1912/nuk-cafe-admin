# ISS-038: Explore is switched off for customers without warning when the street door goes

- Severity: High
- Type: Bug
- Status: Open (2026-11-14)
- Check ID: none (code review)
- Found: 2026-11-14, Claude (code review), `main` at `06bf118`, no browser

## Start state
A branch with a pin, Explore on, and a street door chosen on the ground floor (Explore settings → Street door). The layout has at least two floors.

## Steps to reproduce
Any one of:
1. Remove the ground floor (Floors panel → Remove floor).
2. Select the outer wall that holds the street door and press Delete (it becomes `open` and loses its doors).
3. Select that wall, and in its panel remove the street door.
4. Remove a corner or drag the outline so the street door no longer fits (it is dropped by `fitOpenings()`).

## Expected
Studio protects the street door the same way it already does for moving floors: "Floor not moved: Explore's street door is on the ground floor. Choose no street door in Explore settings first." Or it asks for confirmation and says Explore will be turned off for customers.

## Actual (from code; not tested in a browser)
`useLayoutDraft.change()` sees that `scene.entrance` no longer matches a ground-floor door and silently sets `entrance = null` and `explore = false`. Autosave saves that within about 2 seconds, so customers lose the Explore 3D view of the café. No toast or dialog tells the owner.

## Suspected cause (unverified)
- `app/features/branches/composables/useLayoutDraft.ts`, `change()` (lines 29–39): clears `entrance` and `explore` when `streetDoor()` fails.
- `app/features/branches/components/DesignShopEditor.vue`: `moveFloor()` (line 918) guards; `removeFloor()` (line 940) and `remove()` for an edge (line 492) do not. `EdgePanel.vue` line 153 removes an opening without a check.

## Fix direction
One helper, for example `removesStreetDoor(before, after)`, used by every change that can drop the door (remove floor, delete edge, remove opening, corner delete, outline edits that refit openings). When it would: refuse with the same message as `moveFloor`, or confirm with "Explore will be turned off for customers". Owner decides which (see Open questions). Unit test the helper; e2e: remove the ground floor with a street door chosen.

## How to verify the fix
On staging with Explore on: each of the four steps either is refused with the message, or asks first; after Cancel, Explore settings still show the street door and Explore on. Capture one image per step.

## Open questions
Refuse (like `moveFloor`) or confirm and turn Explore off? Recommendation: refuse, for consistency with `moveFloor`.
