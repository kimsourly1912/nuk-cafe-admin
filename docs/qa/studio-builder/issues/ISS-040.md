# ISS-040: Grid shows only 1–2 lines when zoomed in, and lines look blurry

- Severity: Medium
- Type: Bug
- Status: Open (2026-11-14)
- Check ID: ISS-031 retest (grid)
- Found: 2026-11-14, owner by hand (symptom) + Claude (code review), `main` at `06bf118`

## Start state
Studio, Design view, any floor. Window about 1440 × 900.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Zoom in to the maximum (Ctrl/Cmd + wheel or the zoom menu).
2. Count the grid lines on screen.
3. At 100 % and at 400 %, look at how sharp the grid lines are next to Figma's at the same zoom.

## Expected (reference: Figma)
More, finer grid lines appear as you zoom in, so there are always enough lines to judge position. Lines are sharp, exactly 1 screen pixel. Reference values are `unverified` (from memory, not measured).

## Actual
- Owner (2026-11-14): "when zoom in to the most space it show 2 lines and also not clear in canvas".
- From code: only two fixed levels, 10 cm minor and 100 cm major. Max zoom is 256 px per cm, so the 10 cm lines are 2,560 px apart.
- Lines are drawn in plan units inside nested SVG patterns, so they rarely land on whole screen pixels and look soft.

## Suspected cause
- `app/features/branches/designer/camera.ts`: `GRID_MINOR_CM = 10`, `GRID_MAJOR_CM = 100` (lines 126–127), `ZOOM_LIMITS = { min: 0.02, max: 256 }` (line 37). Confirmed by reading.
- `app/features/branches/components/FloorPlanCanvas.vue` (from line 1485): `plan-grid-minor` pattern nested inside `plan-grid`, strokes with `vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"` inside a pattern. Blur cause `unverified` until seen in a browser: browsers render patterns as image tiles and `non-scaling-stroke` inside a pattern may not behave the same everywhere.

## Fix direction
1. Pure function in `camera.ts`, for example `gridLevels({ scale })` → `{ minor, major, minorOpacity }`: levels 1 → 10 → 100 → 1000 cm; minor = the smallest level at least 8 px apart on screen; major = minor × 10; minor fades in from 8 to 16 px. Unit tests at 0.1, 1, 16 and 256 px per cm.
2. Draw the lines in screen pixels outside the zoom transform: a pure function from camera + canvas size to line positions, rounded to whole pixels + 0.5, `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`. At most about 250 lines per axis. Use a `<canvas>` with `devicePixelRatio` only if this measurably lags.
3. Stronger semantic colour tokens for minor and major lines; check light and dark mode.
4. Keep the grid separate from the snap step (ISS-004).
5. Owner decision: lower max zoom to 32 px per cm (see Open questions).

## How to verify the fix
Unit tests pass. On staging at 2 %, 100 %, 400 % and max zoom: at least about 8 visible lines across the canvas at every zoom; lines look as sharp as Figma's at the same zoom, in light and dark mode. Capture the four zoom levels.

## Open questions
Max zoom: keep 25,600 % (Figma) or lower to 3,200 % (32 px per cm)? Positions are whole centimetres, so above 32 px per cm nothing new is visible. Recommendation: 3,200 %.
