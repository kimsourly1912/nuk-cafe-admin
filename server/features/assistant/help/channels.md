# Channels

**Marketing → Channels** connects the cafe's social channels, so a promotion can be posted there in one click. For now: a **Telegram channel**. A **Facebook Page** is coming (it needs Facebook's approval of the app first); until then, download a promotion's poster from its page and post it on Facebook yourself.

## Connect a Telegram channel

You need a Telegram channel for the cafe, and to be one of its admins.

1. Press **Connect a channel**.
2. **Open Telegram**: choose your channel and add the cafe's bot as an admin. Keep **Post messages** and **Delete messages** on.
3. Copy the message the dialog shows (`/connect` and a code) and **post it in the channel**. The bot reads it and deletes it at once.
4. The dialog shows the channel's name and subscribers: press **Connect channel**.

The code works for 15 minutes, once. Nothing happens after posting it? Check the bot is an admin of the channel that can post messages, then post the code again (or press **New code** once it expired).

## A connected channel

Each channel shows its name, @username, subscribers when it was connected, and who connected it.

- **Bot removed** (red): someone removed the bot or its admin rights in Telegram. Nothing is posted there until you **Reconnect** it (the same steps).
- **Disconnect** (⋮ menu): nothing more is posted there and scheduled posts to it won't go out; posts already in the channel stay. The bot leaves the channel.

## Post a promotion

Open the promotion (Marketing → Promotions, click its name) and press **Post to channels** in **Posts**. Only a live or scheduled promotion can be posted.

1. Tick the **Channels**.
2. Edit the **Caption** (it starts from the promotion's name, discount, description and last day; up to 1,000 characters).
3. **Include the poster**: on, the caption goes under the poster.
4. **When**: **Now**, or **Later** with a date and time (your device's time, up to 30 days ahead).
5. **Post now** or **Schedule post**. The preview shows the post as followers see it.

Every post has an **Order now** button that opens your menu with the offer. Orders placed from it within 24 hours count as **from Telegram posts** in the promotion's **Results**.

## Posts

The promotion's **Posts** list each post: **Sent** (**View post** opens it in Telegram), **Scheduled** (**Cancel** before it goes out), **Sending** or **Retrying** (Telegram is tried again for a while), **Failed** with the reason (**Try again**), **Cancelled** (by you, or because the promotion ended before its time).
