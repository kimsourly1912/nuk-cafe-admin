# Promotions

**Marketing → Promotions** runs discounts on the menu. Customers see the lower price on the menu (the old price struck through and a badge like "20% off"), at checkout and on their order; the counter and Order history show it on each line. Each card shows the poster, the discount, what it covers and when it runs. Tabs **All**, **Live**, **Scheduled**, **Drafts**, **Ended**; search by promotion, item or category name.

## Rules

- **Percent off** (1–100%) or **Amount off** each item ($0.01–$1,000). The discount is taken off each item's price; **add-ons keep their price**. A price never goes below $0.
- **What's included**: **Chosen items** (single items, or whole categories: a category includes everything in it, also items added later; a main category includes its sub-categories) or the **Whole menu**.
- **Never two discounts**: when two promotions cover the same item, customers get the **bigger** discount. The editor says when another promotion also covers your items.
- **When**: all day from the **First day** to the **Last day**, on each branch's own clock, or with **No end date** until you end it.
- **Branches**: every branch, or chosen ones (shown only when the cafe has more than one).
- **Draft**: only you see it. **Scheduled**: published, starts later. **Live**: running now. **Ended**: past its last day or ended by hand.
- Orders keep the price they were placed at: changing or ending a promotion never changes an order already placed.

## Create a promotion

1. Press **New promotion** (or **N**).
2. **Name** (customers see it beside the poster) and an optional **Description**.
3. **Discount**: **Percent off** or **Amount off**, and how much.
4. **What's included**: **Chosen items**, then **Choose items**: tick categories or single items, **Done**. Or **Whole menu**.
5. **When**: the **First day** and **Last day**, or a quick length (**1 day**, **Weekend**, **1 week**, **2 weeks**, **1 month**), or **No end date**.
6. **Poster** (optional): design it in Canva or with an AI tool, then **Upload poster** (square 1080 × 1080 or portrait 1080 × 1350; JPEG, PNG or WebP). Customers see it in **Offers** at the top of the menu.
7. **How customers see it** (beside the form on a computer) shows the badge, the poster and an example price.
8. Press **Publish** to run it (on its first day), or **Save draft** to finish later.

## A promotion's page

Click a promotion's name. It shows the **Results** (orders paid at its price, items sold, the discount given and the sales), the details and the poster (**Download** to post it on social media).

- **Edit**: change anything; a live promotion's new prices reach the menu as soon as you save.
- **Publish**: a draft.
- **End now** (⋮ menu): stops a live or scheduled promotion; prices go back at once. An ended promotion can't be restarted: use **Run again** for a new one with the same settings.
- **Delete draft** (⋮ menu): only drafts; customers never saw them.
