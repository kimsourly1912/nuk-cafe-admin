# Sample data

**Admin → Sample data** (marked **Test**) fills a test environment with realistic cafe data for trying the app. It exists only on local and staging environments, never in production; elsewhere the page says "Sample data is off here".

## Load a sample menu

1. Choose a size: **Small** (12 items), **Standard** (40) or **Large** (150). They include drafts, archived and sold-out items, without photos.
2. Press **Load sample menu**. The steps show their progress.
3. If loading stops, press **Try again** or **Continue loading**: it picks up where it stopped.
4. Then **View menu items** or **View the customer menu**.

## Load branch hours and tables

Press **Load hours and tables**: Mon–Fri 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM, Sat–Sun 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM, and 12 tables (T01–T08 Main floor, P01–P04 Patio) with QR codes. It asks before replacing hours you already set.

## Reset

**Reset menu data…** deletes every menu record (items, categories, options, add-ons, availability rules) and every uploaded image. Type **RESET** to confirm. It can't be undone. Staff and the branch settings aren't touched.
