# Reports

**Reports** in the sidebar shows how the cafe is doing: **Summary**, **Sales by item** and **Order history**. Admins only. Reports only read: nothing is changed from them.

## Choosing the period

The date button at the top left of every report chooses the period: **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **This month**, or two days on the calendar (the first and last day; at most 93 days). The line under it says which branch, its time zone, and when the numbers were read ("Updated 2:35 PM").

A **business day runs from 4:00 AM to 4:00 AM** in the branch's time zone (Asia/Phnom_Penh for Riverside). A payment at 12:30 AM counts on the day before. Moving from one report to another keeps the period.

## Summary

- **Paid sales**: payments taken in the period, whenever the order was placed; with the change against the period before ("vs yesterday").
- **Paid orders**, **Average order** (paid sales ÷ paid orders), **Refunds** (money returned in the period for cancelled paid orders), and **Net sales** = paid sales − refunds.
- **Paid sales by hour** for one day, **by day** for longer periods.
- **Payments**: Cash USD, Cash riel (the riel taken, each at the rate used that day, and its dollar value), KHQR, and the total.
- **Current orders** (only when the period includes today; live): waiting for payment, preparing, ready. Otherwise **Orders placed in this period**.
- **Best sellers**: the top 5 items by quantity sold.
- **Cancelled, not paid**: by the customer, by the cafe, and expired unpaid (not paid within 30 minutes).

## Sales by item

Each item's **Quantity sold**, **Paid sales** (with its add-ons) and **Refunded**, for orders paid (or refunded) in the period. Search with **Find an item**, filter by category, and press a column's name to sort by it. The last row shows the totals of every matching item.

## Order history

Every order placed in the period. Search by **order number** (numbers restart every business day) and filter by **Type**, **Payment** (Unpaid, Paid, Refunded, Not paid) and **Progress** (Waiting for payment, Preparing, Ready, Completed, Cancelled) and **Method**. On a phone, the filters are under **Filters**: choose, then press **Apply filters**.

Press an order's number to open it: its items with options, add-ons and notes, the payment (method, amount, reference, who took it and when, and any money returned), and a timeline of every step with who did it.

## Download CSV and Print

- **Download CSV** saves a spreadsheet file with every matching row (not only the page shown), with the page's filters and order. It opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Print** prints the report in black and white on A4, with the branch, the period, when it was printed and by whom, and how the numbers are counted.

On a phone both are in the **⋯** (Actions) menu.

## Common problems

- **A payment isn't in today's sales**: sales count when the payment is taken, not when the order was placed. An order placed yesterday and paid today counts today.
- **"More than 20,000 orders match"** (CSV): choose a shorter period or add filters.
- **Numbers changed after printing**: today's numbers keep changing until the business day ends at 4:00 AM. Past days don't change, except for refunds, which count on the day the money was returned.
