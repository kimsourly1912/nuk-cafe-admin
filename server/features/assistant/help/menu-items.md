# Menu items

**Menu → Menu items** lists everything customers can order. Switch between the grid and list view with the buttons in the toolbar. Filter by **Category**, search by name, and use the tabs **All**, **Draft**, **Published**, **Archived**.

When some items have no photo, a line under the tabs says how many ("3 menu items have no photo"). **Show them** lists only those (a **No photo** filter; its × removes it). With that filter on, **Save** in an item becomes **Save and next**: it saves and opens the next item without a photo, so you can go through them one after another. The home page's **Set up your cafe** links here too.

A new item is created as a **Draft**: customers don't see it until you publish it.

## Create a menu item

1. Press **New menu item** (or **N**).
2. **Photo** (optional): **Add photo** opens the photo editor. Choose a photo (drop it in or paste it on a computer; on a phone, **Take photo** or **Choose photo**), then frame it. The tabs **Menu card** (square) and **Item page** (4:3) choose which one you're framing: drag the photo inside the frame to move it, and zoom with the slider (or the mouse wheel, or the arrow keys and + and −). The part left out shows dimmed around the frame. The item page follows the menu card until you frame it yourself; **Reset** starts that view again. On the right you see both as customers will; clicking one frames it. The item page keeps the whole photo, with the photo's background colour around it, unless filling the frame cuts very little. The **rotate** buttons beside the tabs turn a sideways photo. **Use photo** closes the editor and the photo uploads: it shows on the form dimmed, with a spinner, and **Save** waits until it has uploaded. You can keep editing meanwhile, and **Edit photo** opens it again as you left it. If the upload fails, the form says so: **Try again**, or **Cancel** to keep the photo the item had; **Save** waits until you choose. Closing the form while a photo uploads asks first. Very large photos (from new phones) are made smaller as they open, so they work on phones with little memory too. The photo goes on the menu when you save the item. **Edit photo** also opens a saved photo later as you left it. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 25 MB; a small photo is warned about, since it may look blurry on the item page.
3. **Name** and **Category** (required). The category must be one without subcategories: choose the subcategory, not its main category.
4. **Description** (optional).
5. **Options and prices**: see below. Without option sets the item has one version: enter its **Price**.
6. **Add-ons** (optional): **Add add-on group** to offer extras from the Add-ons library.
7. **Availability** (optional): choose rules to limit when it's sold. None means whenever the branch is open. Its category's rules apply too.
8. Press **Create** to save it as a draft, or **Create and publish** to put it on the menu at once.
9. A draft can be published later: **Save and publish** in its editor, or **⋮ → Publish** in the list.

If publishing is refused (for example no version is switched on with a price, or the category has subcategories), the item is still saved as a draft and a message says why.

On a phone the editor shows one section at a time (Details, Prices, Add-ons, Availability) with Save at the bottom.

## Options and prices (versions)

- **Add option set** adds a set from the Options library, like Size or Temperature. An item can have up to 2.
- Each combination of values is a **version**, for example "Hot / Small", "Iced / Large". The grid shows one row per version with a switch (**Sell …**) and a price.
- Switch off the versions you don't sell. A version that's on needs a price.
- Removing an option set rebuilds the grid; check the prices again.

## Add-ons on an item

- **Add add-on group** picks a group from the Add-ons library. Up to 10 groups; use the arrows to order them.
- By default the group's rules (At least / At most) and prices come from the library. Turn on **Own rules for this item** to set different rules for this item only.
- Change an add-on's price here to charge a different price on this item only.

## Publish, unpublish, archive, restore

From the item's **⋮** menu in the list:

- **Publish**: puts a draft on the customer menu. It needs at least one version switched on with a price.
- **Unpublish**: takes it off the menu and back to draft.
- **Archive**: hides it and makes it read-only. Past orders keep it. Several at once: **Select**, tick the items, **Archive selected**.
- **Publish several at once**: **Select**, tick the items, **Publish selected** (only drafts are published; the button says how many). Items that can't be published stay ticked, and the message says why.
- **Restore** (on an archived item): back to draft, editable again.

**Undo:** Archive and Unpublish happen at once, without a question. The message that appears has **Undo** for a few seconds: it puts the item back exactly as it was (published or draft, in the same place in its category). Missed it? Restore the item from the **Archived** tab (it comes back as a draft).

To mark something out of stock for today, don't unpublish it: staff use **Sold out** in the counter app.

## See an item as customers do

A published item has **View on menu** in its form (and in its row's ⋮ menu): it opens your customer menu in a new tab with that item open. A draft or an archived item isn't on the menu, so the button is greyed out until you publish it. The menu can take up to a minute to show a change you just saved.

## Change the order of items in a category

Customers see a category's items in the order you set here, for example best sellers first.

1. Press **Reorder** in the toolbar (or the **R** key). If you're filtering by a category, it opens on that category.
2. Choose the **Category**.
3. Drag an item by its handle, or use its up and down arrows.
4. Press **Save order**. Archived items aren't listed and keep their place.

If someone changed one of these items at the same moment, nothing is moved: press **Reload**, then move them again.

## Edit a menu item

Click the item's name (or **⋮ → Edit**), change it, then **Save**. Changes to a published item show on the customer menu at once.

## Unsaved changes kept in this browser

If the browser closes, the tablet runs out of battery or you are signed out before saving, what you typed isn't lost. The next time you open the same item (or **New menu item**) in this browser, it says **Unsaved changes from** a time:

- **Restore** puts your changes back in the form. They aren't saved yet: check them and press **Save**.
- **Discard** throws them away.

If someone saved the item after you typed (an amber message), restoring puts your changes over theirs: check every section before saving.

## Duplicate an item

To add a similar item (an Iced Latte after the Latte), open the item's **⋮** menu and choose **Duplicate**. **New menu item** opens with everything filled in from it (options and prices, add-ons, availability), named "Latte (copy)". The photo isn't copied: add one. Change what differs, then **Create** (a draft) or **Create and publish**. Nothing is saved until you do.

## Someone else saved the same item

If someone saves the item while you have it open, your **Save** is refused: "Someone else saved this menu item". Nothing you typed is lost. Press **Reload**: their changes come into the form, and yours stay on top. If you both changed the same part (for example the prices), yours are kept and the message names it: check that part, then **Save**. If they archived the item meanwhile, restore it from the list and open it again: your changes are offered back. Your changes are kept only in this browser, for 7 days, and only you see them. **Log out** removes them all.

## Common problems

- **"Switch on and price at least one version before publishing."**: open the item, switch on a version in Options and prices and give it a price, save, then publish.
- **"That category has sub-categories; put the item in one of them."**: choose one of its subcategories as the category.
- **"Choose an active option set / add-on group / category"**: something the item uses was archived. Remove it from the item or restore it in its library.
- **"This menu item is archived. Restore it from the list to edit it."**: archived items are read-only; **Restore** first.
- **An option set can't be added**: the item already has 2, or the set is archived.
- **The item doesn't show on the customer menu**: see "When does a customer see an item?" in the menu overview.
