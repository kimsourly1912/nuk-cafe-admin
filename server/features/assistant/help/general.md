# Using the admin portal

The admin portal is where admins set up NUK Cafe (the menu, staff, the branch and payments) and see how sales went (reports). Customers order on the cafe's website; staff take payment and prepare orders in the counter app.

## The sidebar

- **Dashboard**: the start page.
- **Menu**: Menu items, Categories, Options, Add-ons, Availability.
- **Reports**: Summary, Sales by item, Order history.
- **Admin**: Staff, Branch, Payments, Telegram, and Sample data (test environments only).

On a phone, open the sidebar with the menu button at the top left.

## Saving your work

- Forms save only when you press **Save** (or **Create**, **Save changes**). **Ctrl+Enter** (⌘+Enter on a Mac) saves the open form.
- If you close a form, leave the page, sign out or reload with changes you haven't saved, the portal asks "Discard unsaved changes?" first. Choose **Cancel** to go back to your form.
- You can close a form while it's saving: the save continues. If it then fails, the message offers **Reopen** with what you typed.
- A green message in the corner confirms each save. A red one says what went wrong.

## "Changed by someone else"

Two admins may edit the same thing at the same time. The second save is refused with a message like "…was changed by someone else. Reload it and try again."

1. Press **Reload** in the message (where it's offered). Your typed changes stay on screen, on top of the latest version.
2. Check the result, then save again.

Where there's no Reload button, close the form, open it again and redo the change.

## Lists

- **Search**: type in the search box; the list filters as you type. Press **/** to jump to it.
- **Tabs** such as All, Active, Archived filter by status and show counts.
- **Select**: a button in the toolbar starts selecting several rows for a bulk action (for example **Archive selected**). Escape leaves it.
- **Archive** hides something from customers without deleting it; **Restore** brings it back. Nothing on the menu is deleted, so past orders keep their details.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** to see them all. The main ones: **/** search, **N** new item on list pages, **Ctrl+Enter** save, **Escape** close a dialog.

## Other things you may see

- **You're offline**: a banner shows when the connection drops. Wait for it to come back before saving.
- **Another tab**: signing in or out in one tab applies to every open tab. Saving in one tab refreshes the same lists in your other tabs.
- **Light and dark mode**: the sun/moon button in the sidebar.
- **Change password**: from the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar. A new staff account must change its temporary password at the first sign-in.
- **Signed out suddenly**: your session ended (expired, or your access changed). Sign in again.

## This assistant

The assistant answers questions about this portal and links to its pages. It can't change anything for you, and it can make mistakes: check before you act. It has a daily limit per admin; when it's reached, it's available again after midnight (cafe time).
