# Cafe profile

**Admin → Cafe profile** sets the cafe's **name** and **logo**. Customers see them at the top of the menu, on Review order and on their orders; staff see them on the counter and in the admin's sidebar. The browser tab shows the name too.

## Change the name or the logo

1. Change the **Cafe name** (at most 80 characters).
2. Under **Logo**, press **Upload logo** and pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image. A square image works best: it's shown small, beside the name. **Replace logo** picks another one; **Remove** goes back to the coffee cup shown when there's no logo.
3. The **Preview** shows the name and logo as they'll appear.
4. Press **Save changes** (top right on a computer, the bar at the bottom on a phone or tablet).

If someone else saved the profile while you were editing, the page says so: press **Reload** (what you typed stays), then save again.

## The web address

**Web address** is the menu's address (for example …/c/nuk). The admin and the counter are under it, and the table QR codes open it. Only the platform team can change it; an old address keeps sending people to the new one.
