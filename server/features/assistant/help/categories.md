# Categories

**Menu → Categories** holds the menu's sections on two levels: **main categories** (like "Coffee") on the left, and beside them a banner and the **subcategories** (like "Hot coffee", "Iced coffee") of the one you click. On a phone the main categories come first, and tapping one opens it; the buttons above it switch to another, and the back arrow at the top returns to the list. **New category** is the **+** button there. The customer menu shows a section per main category and a sub-section per subcategory, in this order.

**The one rule:** menu items go in a category that has **no subcategories**. A main category holds either menu items or subcategories, never both, and archived subcategories count: while it has any, it takes no menu items (delete them first).

Tabs **All**, **Active**, **Archived** filter both panels; the search finds categories by name or description. Each main category says what it holds (subcategories or items) and how many items in all.

## Create a category

1. Press **New category** (or **N**).
2. **Name** (required; unique at its level).
3. **Parent category**: leave **None (main category)** for a main category, or choose a main category to make a subcategory.
4. **Description** (optional).
5. **Availability** (optional): rules that apply to every item in it. Items can add their own rules too.
6. Press **Create**.

Shortcut: click a main category, then **Add subcategory** (or **⋮ → Add subcategory** on its banner) opens the form with that parent chosen. On a main category that already holds menu items, the new subcategory takes them: the form says how many will move, and customers keep seeing them.

## Edit a category

For a main category, click it, then press **Edit details** on its banner. For a subcategory, click its name or use **⋮ → Edit**. **⋮ → Add menu item** starts a new item in that category; **⋮ → Manage items** opens Menu items filtered to it. A main category with subcategories takes its items in those.

## Turn a category with items into one with subcategories

Add a subcategory to it: click it, press **Add subcategory**, name it and press **Create**. All its menu items move into the new subcategory at once, archived ones too, and customers keep seeing them. Add more subcategories, then move items between them from each item's **Category**.

## Change the order

1. Press **Reorder** (or **R**).
2. Drag rows by the handle, or use **Move up** / **Move down**. Subcategories move within their main category; click another main category to reorder its subcategories too. On a phone it's one level at a time: the main categories first, and **›** steps into a category's subcategories.
3. Press **Save order**, or **Discard** to cancel.
4. Press **Done** (the Reorder button) to leave.

## Archive, restore and delete

- **⋮ → Archive** (a main category's **⋮** is on its banner): customers stop seeing it and its items. The items stay and come back with it. Archiving a main category also archives its active subcategories.
- **Restore**: brings it back at the end of its level. Restoring a main category asks whether to restore its subcategories too.
- A subcategory can't be restored while its main category is archived: restore the main category first.
- Several at once: **Select** (or **S**), tick main categories and subcategories (click a main category to see its subcategories; the ticks stay, and on a phone **Main categories** steps back), then **Archive selected** or **Restore selected**.
- **⋮ → Delete permanently** (on an archived category): removes it for good. It asks first, and it can't be restored. Only an **empty** category nothing was ordered from can be deleted: no menu items and no subcategories in it (archived ones count too: delete them first, in **Menu items** and here), never used for an order, and in no promotion. Otherwise the action is greyed out and says why, and the category stays archived so your reports keep it.

## Common problems

- **"The menu items in that category changed."** (when creating a subcategory): someone added or removed one meanwhile. The form shows the new number; check it and press **Create** again.
- **"There is already a category named … here."**: names are unique among categories with the same parent.
- **"Its parent category is archived. Restore the parent first."**
- **"This category still has menu items (archived ones count too)."**: delete or move those items first (Menu items → Archived tab), then delete the category.
- **"Menu items were ordered from this category, so it stays archived"**: a category customers ordered from is never deleted; keep it archived.
- **"These categories were changed by someone else."** (when saving the order): press **Reload**. Your order stays on screen; check it and save again.
