# Platform support

**Admin → Platform support** lets the platform team (the people who run the app for every cafe) look at this cafe's admin when you need help. Only owners can allow it, and nobody from the platform team can open your cafe without it.

## Allow support

1. Press **Allow for 24 hours**, then confirm.
2. While it's on, a strip at the top of every admin page says until when.
3. Tell the platform team what you need: they open the cafe from their own console.

They see what you see in the admin: the menu, orders, reports, staff and settings. They **can't change anything**: every save is refused. They can't download reports, use the assistant or upload photos either.

## End it

Press **End now** on Platform support (or **Manage** in the strip). Anyone looking is shut out at once. Otherwise it ends by itself after 24 hours.

## Who looked

**History** lists each time support was allowed, who allowed and ended it, and who from the platform team opened the cafe and when. Every page they opened is also in the cafe's audit history.
